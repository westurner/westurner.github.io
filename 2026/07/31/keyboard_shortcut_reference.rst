
Keyboard Shortcut Reference
===========================

Browser shortcuts
------------------

+-----------------+----------------------------------------+------------------------------+
| Shortcuts       | Description                            | Tools that support shortcut  |
+-----------------+----------------------------------------+------------------------------+
| Ctrl-L          | Go to Address Bar                      | FF, Chrome, File browsers    |
+-----------------+----------------------------------------+------------------------------+
| Ctrl-D          | Create Bookmark                        | FF, Chrome                   |
+-----------------+----------------------------------------+------------------------------+
| Ctrl-Shift-I    | Open Devtools and Inspect Element      | FF, Chrome, vscode           |
+-----------------+----------------------------------------+------------------------------+
| Ctrl-Shift-C    | Open Devtools and Inspect Element      | FF, Chrome, vscode           |
+-----------------+----------------------------------------+------------------------------+

Terminal shortcuts
--------------------
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
| Shortcuts                    | Description               | Tools that support shortcut   |
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
| Ctrl-PgDn (Ctrl-Fn-Down)     | Next tab                  | FF, Chrome, Terminal          |
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
| Shift-Home (Shift-Fn-Left)   | Select from here to Home  | Most                          |
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
| Shift-End (Shift-Fn-Right)   | Select from here to End   | Most                          |
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
| Home, Fn-Left                | Home                      | Most                          |
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
| End, Fn-Right                | End                       | Most                          |
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
| PgDn, Fn-Down                | PgDn                      | Most                          |
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
| PgDn, Fn-Up                  | PgUp                      | Most                          |
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
| Ctrl-C                       |                           | Terminal                      |
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
| Ctrl-D                       | EOF                       | Terminal                      |
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
| Ctrl-Z                       | Send to background        | Terminal w/ `jobs` control    |
+------------------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
